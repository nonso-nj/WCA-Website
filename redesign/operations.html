Giving to WCA

Thank you for supporting the work of the gospel at WCA. Choose how you’d like to give.

Ways to give

Why we give

We believe it is a priestly duty to give, and giving has always been tied to a promise, in both the old and the new covenant.

So with each offering, tithe or gift to the Lord, pray over it, acknowledge that the gift is for the Lord, and then release it in faith. Give cheerfully, and as it lies within your capacity.

We would also love to hear your testimony! Share your testimony

01 · Interac e-Transfer

Give by Interac

Send a gift from your online banking or banking app.

Send to winnipegchristianassembly@gmail.com

In the message, add your name, what the gift is for (such as Tithe, Offering, Welfare or Missions) and a short prayer point.

Give automatically every week or month
  1. Sign in to your online banking or banking app.
  2. Choose Interac e-Transfer, then Send money, and add WCA as a recipient using the address above.
  3. Enter the amount and a message with your name, what the gift is for and a short prayer point.
  4. Look for Recurring, Repeat or Schedule, and choose how often: weekly, every two weeks or monthly. Pick a start date.
  5. Review and confirm. Each gift then goes out on its own, and you can change or stop it at any time in your banking.

Not every bank offers repeating e-Transfers. If yours doesn’t, a monthly reminder on your phone works too.

Giving questions

Where does my money go?

“Bring all the tithes into the storehouse, that there may be food in My house.” (Malachi 3:10, NKJV) Your giving goes to that. We do not have a paid pastorship: every gift goes to the wellbeing of the house of God and the expansion of His work, as He leads.

What should I include with an Interac transfer?

Your name, what the gift is for (such as Tithe, Offering, Welfare or Missions) and a short prayer point. Your name and the purpose help us record the gift correctly, and we pray over every prayer point we receive.

Can I set up automatic giving?

Yes. Many banks let you schedule a repeating Interac e-Transfer; see “Give automatically every week or month” above. Card giving with automatic monthly gifts is coming soon.

Will I get a tax receipt?

Winnipeg Christian Assembly is a non-profit organization rather than a registered charity, so we can’t issue official tax receipts. If you’d like a record of your giving for the year, just ask.

Who can answer a giving question?

Call 431-373-7299 or email winnipegchristianassembly@gmail.com.
