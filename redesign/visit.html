What to expect

Sunday service

Worship, prayer and the Word

A typical Sunday moves through worship, prayer and the Word. We like to leave room for the Holy Spirit to move, so some Sundays turn out to be all worship, all prayer, or mostly exhortation. Those are our favourites, when the Lord interrupts our schedule. Even so, we usually finish at the usual time.

Ages 0–12

Kids’ classes

Children aged 0–12 have their own classes from 10:15 to 11:30 a.m. during the Sunday service.

Getting here

Parking & access

There’s parking in the building and street parking nearby. The building is accessible.

Good to know

How long is the service?

Sunday service runs from 10:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. Wednesday Bible study runs from 7:00 to 9:00 p.m.

Where do you meet?

Sunday services are at 90 Ashland Avenue, Winnipeg, Manitoba R3L 1K6. Wednesday Bible study meets in the Isbister Building at the University of Manitoba. Contact us for details: 431-373-7299.

Is there something for children?

Yes. Kids aged 0–12 have their own classes from 10:15 to 11:30 a.m. during the Sunday service. A greeter can show you where to go.

Where do I park?

There’s parking in the building, and street parking nearby.

Is the building accessible?

Yes, the building is accessible.

First-time guest connection

We’d be glad to say hello.

Let us know you’re thinking of visiting, and we can look out for you or answer a question beforehand.

We look forward to meeting you.
