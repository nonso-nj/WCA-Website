What is God doing II – The Four Watches

January 8, 2023Bro Samuel AgboolaWhat is God doing? New year 2023

Listen

Wherefore the rather, brethren, give diligence to make your calling and election sure: for if ye do these things, ye shall never fall: [11] For so an entrance shall be ministered unto you abundantly into the everlasting kingdom of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ. [12] Wherefore I will not be negligent to put you always in remembrance of these things, though ye know them, and be established in the present truth. 

2 Peter 1:10-12 KJV 

God also has time allocated for everything He does. The scriptures, from Genesis down to Revelation, all speak of the plan of God in the earth. While God exists in timelessness, He has a schedule for all the happenings on the earth. There are those who live in the yesterday. There are those who are present with God's timing on the earth (walking in the present truth). There are also those who are able to pull resources from ages to come and enjoy it now. This all has to do with the faith and understanding of the individual. Yet God's desire is that we are all, at the minimum, walking in the present truth. As we are faithful in this, God opens doors into ages and experiences that are beyond our time as experienced by saints of Old like David, Moses, Elijah, Abraham, etc.
