Wisdom and Foolishness

June 3, 2018Sis Kemi Enumah

Listen

Audio coming soon

It is easy to see wisdom as being smart, However, there is more to wisdom that the book of proverb speaks about. Wisdom is an entity, the person of Christ, Proverbs 1:20-23 correlates with Joel 2:28-29 and Matthew 11:15-19. Proverbs 8 further gives insight into the person of Wisdom, Christ. Paul calls Christ the wisdom of God, 1 Corinthians 1:24. Proverbs 8 speaks of wisdom leading in the way of righteousness; finding wisdom means finding life. Jesus said “I am the way, the truth and the life” John 14:6. Wisdom is Jesus Christ, and as we seek Him, He pours out His spirit upon us. Wisdom and understanding go hand in hand. Proverbs 8:14. You cannot have wisdom and not have understanding. Wisdom is the Jesus Christ, Understanding is the person of the Holy Spirit in believers, John 14:26.

Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding.

Proverbs 4:7 KJV

You cannot have Jesus and not have the Holy Spirit in you. We are to seek and cry earnestly for wisdom and understanding. Wisdom cries out to us Proverbs 1:20 and in a similar way, no one came come to God till He first draws us, John 6:44, we respond by seeking wisdom, Psalms 27:8. The Lord is the rewarder of them that diligently seek Him. Hebrews 44:6 This reward is Himself, Proverbs 2:6.

Why do we need Wisdom?
Wisdom allows us to prepare for the coming of Christ. In the previous meditation, we considered our change. That change cannot come unless we have understanding and wisdom.

Behold, thou desirest truth in the inward parts: and in the hidden part thou shalt make me to know wisdom.

Psalm 51:6 KJV

David understood that he needs wisdom to be changed, Psalm 51:3-6, There is a change that wisdom brings about that is very needed.
Wisdom is hidden, 1 Corinthians 2:7-8, Proverbs 25:2. Matthew 7:24-25 speaks of two men, one who hears the word and does it, the one who built his house on the rock, and one who does not do them, the one who builds his house on sand. Matthew 25:1-10 speaks about 2 sets of 5 virgins, one set wise and the other foolish. It is time that distinguishes the wise and the foolish. Revelations 14:4-5, describes virgins as those who followed the Lamb wherever He went, caring for the things of the Lord, 1 Corinthians 7:34. It is important to note that the foolish virgins were not unbelievers or carnally-minded Christians. They all went out to meet the Lord and were all waiting for the Lord, they must have all been kingdom-minded and perhaps maybe believers who have heeded the call to come out of the backslidden church system, Revelations 18:4. We are often very quick to put foolishness afar off, and we often don’t regard some of our deeds as foolish. The only thing that distinguished them from the wise was the extra oil, which was most likely hidden, they did not expect the bridegroom to tarry. The foolish might have considered the need to carry extra oil if it was not hidden. That oil signifies wisdom, it is hidden. Consider the wise man who builds his house on the rock and the foolish on the sand. Though it is the strongest foundation you can have, it is rather expensive to build your house on a rock, since it requires breaking through thick masses of rocks and it is very time-consuming. The foolish man just went on the sand and did a quick foundation. According to the world’s standard, he must have been quite smart, he saved money, he didn’t spend a lot of time. However, the foundation of the wise man was wisdom. In the end, their houses might even look alike. The foolish man might be someone who is considered ‘on-fire’ for the Lord- giving, prophesying, preaching the word. You can be foolish and your lamp is still burning. Wisdom is not very obvious in the individual who possesses it. Also, Wisdom is not something you get at once; you grow in wisdom, Luke 2: 52, says Jesus grew in Wisdom. The deeper we press into God, the more realms of wisdom we explore.

There are two kinds of wisdom, the worldly wisdom and the wisdom from above.

Who is a wise man and endued with knowledge among you? let him shew out of a good conversation his works with meekness of wisdom. [14] But if ye have bitter envying and strife in your hearts, glory not, and lie not against the truth. [15] This wisdom descendeth not from above, but is earthly, sensual, devilish. [16] For where envying and strife is, there is confusion and every evil work. [17] But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy. [18] And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace of them that make peace.

James 3:13-18 KJV

1 Corinthians 1:18-31,2:6-8 also considers the wisdom of this world as foolishness in the sight of God and that God delights in using foolish things of the world to confound the wise, that no flesh glory in His presence. The wisdom of God is hidden to the foolish, 1 Corinthians 2:6-8. Sometimes, Christians operate in the wisdom of this world, e.g. advising a person to divorce another because of unfaithfulness, rather than encouraging prayer and forgiveness is worldly wisdom; knowing that God had not ordained divorce, but Moses permitted it because of the hardness of hearts by the children of Israel. Matthew 19:4-8. The wisdom of this world generates strife and wickedness, but that of God is peaceable. We must check ourselves, it there is strife or envying, the wisdom contained there is not of God.

What is foolishness?

  1. When you are a hearer of the Lord and not a doer. Matthew 7:25-26. It is of necessity that we hear and test and practices the word of God we receive. 1Thessalonians 5:21. We must actively put into practice what we learn of God. From James 1:22-25, the mirror is the word of God, 2 Corinthians 3:18, we must be active doers of the word, to be changed and not let cares of the word choke out the word. Matthew 13:22. When your joy is dependent on situations and not the Lord, that is foolishness.
  2. When you walk in the flesh, not after the Spirit. Romans 8:1
  3. When you despise correction. Proverbs 3:11-12. Lack of willingness to accept the chastening of the Lord or wise counsel is foolishness. Proverbs 5:23, Hebrews 12:8.
  4. Starting out your life in the spirit and trying to be perfect in the flesh. Galatians 3:1-9. When we start out as believers, we often start out with a dependency on God and often times down the line, we switch to trying to use our strength to please God. That is foolishness. Trying to do anything with your own strength, without depending on the Holy Spirit is foolishness, seeing that we have nothing good to offer God, but that which he gives us. Psalm 16:2. An example is Abraham, who was promised an heir and listened to Sarah and got a son by his own strength, Ishmael. God had to tell him that Ishmael was not his own choice. He was the result of the foolishness of Abraham, Genesis 16:1-6. Isaac who came later was solely on God’s strength, Genesis 17:18-21.
  5. Foolishness is when you do not obey the truth. Galatians 3:1-3.

6. Foolishness is when we are not prudent, when you do not make use of your time well. Proverbs 12:23. It is Gods will that we use our time wisely. Ephesians 5:16. God is willing to help us if we ask and receive His help. Not allowing God to help as well is foolishness.

7. If you are wise in the world, there is a high tendency that you are foolish in Gods eyes. 1 Corinthians 3:18-20. To be carnally (earthly) minded is death, Romans 8:6-7. Things that people in the world do, to God, its foolishness and to them, what we do is foolishness. Trying to blend into the world is foolishness also. Romans 12:2. Even Jesus’ own people rejected him, they were foolish. John 1:11-13, Matthew 11:15-19

8. Someone who despises knowledge. Hosea 4:6, Job 36:12, Proverbs 10:21. Proverbs 1:19-21. When one just does not seek knowledge, that is foolishness. People are destroyed because they despise knowledge.

9. When people decide to stay foolish. The consequences for this is seen in Proverbs 1:25-33.

Why do we need to harken unto this? Trials will come, testing will come and it is through wisdom and understanding that we can be sustained through trials and temptations that come. It is of necessity that we look inward and ask the Lord to show us our foolishness. We cannot be like Abner, 2 Samuel 3:33-34, who died a fools’ death, being fully armed with a sword, which could symbolize the word of God. It is wisdom that teaches us to walk in righteousness and to know and use that which has been given to us. 1 Corinthians 2:12.

To-do: Read the book of proverbs, 1 chapter a day and the book of James. These are key books expounding more on wisdom and foolishness.

And they that be wise shall shine as the brightness of the firmament; and they that turn many to righteousness as the stars for ever and ever.

Daniel 12:3 KJV
