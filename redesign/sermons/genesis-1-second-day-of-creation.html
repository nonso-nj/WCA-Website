Second Day of creation (Genesis 1)

September 22, 2018Bro Samuel AgboolaDays Of creation

Listen

Audio coming soon

We had established in the last meditation, that the entirety of Genesis 1 is the unveiling or bringing to birth of the new creation man. It shows the steps that bring to light the emergence of the new creation man, the man who will have dominion over all creation, the man in the image and the likeness of God. Genesis 1:26-27. Psalm 8:4-6 says

“What is man, that thou art mindful of him? and the son of man, that thou visitest him? [5] For thou hast made him a little lower than the angels [Elohim}, and hast crowned him with glory and honour. [6] Thou madest him to have dominion over the works of thy hands; thou hast put all things under his feet:”.

Psalm 8:4-6 KJV

This was a position meant for Adam, but he never got to it, the condition for him to get there was to eat the tree of life, Genesis 3:22-24. That which he had missed out on, we have an opportunity to partake, In fact, the tree of life and the river of life is inside of you. Revelation 2:7, 1 John 5:5. The new creation man is a mobile garden of Eden, Christ is the tree and the water of life. Isaiah 58:11, John 4:14. You are a completely new species of Gods creation, 2 Corinthians 5:17. You are a mystery revealed, Gods divine mystery hid throughout the ages, which the prophets sought to understand. The fact that we were picked from the gentile world makes it a deeper mystery, even to the Israelites. Ephesians 3:1-10. Do not consider yourself a mistake, Psalm 8:4-6, you are divinely thought of and He has now given you His Holy Spirit. You are God’s workmanship and He is the one doing the work in you, which He will surely complete. Ephesians 2:10. God’s workmanship is flawless and blameless. You might not see yourself as such and people around you may not see you that way, but it is God who assesses you and places you in the position you are. The glorious thing you can do that will gladden the Father’s heart is to continually address yourself the way He addresses you. You honour Him by doing so. If it is in the scripture, then it is true, believe it.

Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ: [4] According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before him in love:

Ephesians 1:3-4 KJV

Until you begin to see yourself the way He sees you, you can never become it. You can do whatever you want, be it penance, you can afflict yourself, but you will never move an inch closer to what God has for you. However, if in the simplicity of faith, you take the word of God as it is and call it over your life and believe it over your life, you will move at a greater speed and arrive at a destination. He has given you His word and this Word is the same Word He used to frame the world, Hebrews 11:3, and that Word cannot fail. Blessed is the one who believes what is written for he shall see the performance of it. Luke 1:45. The just shall live by faith. Considering Genesis 1:1-3, we said previously that the essence of the new creation man is light. The fertilized egg that caused the emergence of the new creation man is light, hence, whatever comes forth must be perfect light. God is light, 1 John 1:5. He calls this light to come forth from the dark regions of our heart. He took the new creation material from the fallen Adam that was without form and void and darkness filled his heart. When he says Lord Jesus, be My saviour, instantly, light breaks forth into that dark region. 2 Corinthians 4:6. However, this light is amid the darkness. This darkness is your minds belief, contradictions that you may face (lust, pride), struggles and weaknesses of the flesh, but despite this, there is the light. The light may appear little, but keep watching it, it will grow into a day star. 2 Peter 1:19. Watching it entails reading the bible, whether you understand it or not, ask for understanding, the more you do this, the more this light grows till it becomes a Sun. As we said in the previous meditation, 6 circles of evening and morning must occur till we come to the fullness where there is no more night, a perpetual day. As we said, entering into the seventh day is a corporate affair.

Brethren, I count not myself to have apprehended: but this one thing I do, forgetting those things which are behind, and reaching forth unto those things which are before … Nevertheless, whereto we have already attained, let us walk by the same rule, let us mind the same thing.

Philippians 3:13,16

We should have the attitude of Paul. Wherever you have attained, the rule is the same. The only thing that goes against the rule is to turn back from being consistent. Hebrews 10:38. If all you know is John 3:16, hold on to it faithfully and the Lord will do quick work and cut it short in righteousness, Romans 9:28. For a thousand years to Him is as a day, 2 Peter 3:8. That is, what was meant to take 1000 years can be finished in 1 day. If you are already at the 6th evening and morning and you are about to press on, you will enter into it. However, when it comes to the time of breaking forth into the fulness, you must wait for your brethren. This is where intercession comes in. Just like in the time of Pentecost, Acts 2, the day must fully come, no one is going ahead of his brother. Joshua and Caleb were ready to enter the promised land 40 years ahead of time. They trusted God and believed God, but as long as those that had unbelief were dying, they could not go in. If Joshua and Caleb had known this by revelation, before they went to spy the land, they may have prayed perhaps and bound the spirit of unbelief. They may have even agreed with their brethren that they should keep quiet while they speak. However, their brethren kept speaking negatively, that they called themselves grasshoppers. The next most tragic moment happened after the fall, the bible said they wept all through the night. God called it a breach and He swore that these will not enter His rest, only Joshua and Caleb did, 40 years after. He said as truly as I live, the knowledge of My glory shall cover the earth. The Israelites were supposed to be the ones to reveal the knowledge of God, but they counted themselves unworthy and He took time to bury them in the wilderness. Numbers 14:1-24. What should we learn from this? Only declare what God says, never entertain unbelief or drawback. Say what He says about you. Wherever you are, be faithful and you will make it. Genesis 1 said He created light and divided it from darkness, He gave the man to choose. Anywhere you see God dividing something, it is Him giving a choice. That is in harmony with His Divine nature. As a born-again child of God, you have the right to make a choice. When you were in darkness, there was no choice. Now, If you choose light and follow after light, you live. It is your birthright as a born-again child of God to choose.

That is what differentiates man from other creatures, you can choose to follow God or to go back to your old life, to serve Him fully or partially. He said I place before you, life and death and He counsels you to choose life. Deuteronomy 30:15-20. Each person will have different temptations. God will allow them because He must give you the right to make a choice, this is the first thing you encounter when you become born again, choice. Jesus asked Peter after he had gone fishing again if he loved him, this is because he had to make a choice if he would choose Jesus or his trade. John 21. Never you think it will not come, it will always come until we reach the fullness. We must resolve at all times to choose God, to choose light. It might cost you friends, family or even your life. He will make the choice clear, that you will make an intelligent choice. Hence it is said that whatsoever is born of God cannot willfully sin because the Seed of God is within Him. 1 John 3:9. The seed of God is light, you cannot choose darkness. God called light day and the darkness He called night. Whatsoever you call, you have mastery over it. Once a man can begin to assign names to these things, he has come into a level of conquest, of victory. May this be our portion. Because you have called darkness a name, you govern it. Whatever name you call them, that is what they are. Darkness is night and it is not a time for a man. Because of the limitations the night brings, you don’t love the night, you love the day. You gain mastery over it, you can separate the two of them and choose light. Once you choose light, you become a child of the day. 1 Thessalonians 5:5. He trains you to use your senses to discern between good and evil. Hebrews 5:14. We must exercise our spiritual senses to discern good and evil, this is by taking in the word of God, Romans 12:2.
In Genesis 1:6-8, God introduced another thing that is going to bring about a clear distinction, the firmament. The firmament reveals His power, where He stands upon, Psalm 150:1. He brings His government over your life. You begin to feel the weight of it. Hitherto, it has been a choice, but when He has seen that there is faithfulness in the right choice, He installs His government. It is a beautiful thing and it makes choices very easy. You are empowered to make goodly choices, to choose light. This firmament separates the waters above from the waters beneath. When one is born again, the Spirit comes alive and the soul remains, the firmament separates between the Spirit and soul and sets His government over your soul. Ezekiel 1:22-26 describes this.

You are a living creature; the church of the firstborn is the living creature. This is where He sets His throne. His government brings upon you a constraint and it teaches you how to kill self and the enemy. It equips you with the armoury to live victoriously over sin. You do not only choose now, but you choose with the mindset to destroy the enemy. He empowers you to do so, His government is the power of His might that strengthens you. Ephesians 6:10-12. This happens when we get baptised in the Holy Spirit. You might have been previously led by the reactions of men, but now, you are being led by the Spirit of God. Romans 8:14. The ‘waters above’ is the Spirit and the ‘water below’ is your soul. You will see later, that the water below separates into the land and the sea and the both must bring forth. As you allow His government to be upon you, the earth will bring forth goodly things and the sea as well. This will be the topic of another meditation. However, know that the second day is the day of His rulership. He lowers His hand upon your head. That head is your mind, the love of Christ constrains you, you cannot do what you want, He is your Lord. This is because, at the final stage, He will stamp out everything that is of the old, that you will not even remember sin.


Thy people shall be willing in the day of thy power, in the beauties of holiness from the womb of the morning: thou hast the dew of thy youth. Psalm 110:3 KJV

The Grace of the Lord be with your Spirit.
