Under the Shadow of His Wings

April 13, 2025Bro Chidera BisongCombatting Divination and Enchantments against the millennial temple

Download sermon notes (PDF)

Psalm 91:1 KJVS
He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.

Experience the Power of Divine Covering: "Under the Shadow of His Wings" – A Message on Overcoming Spiritual Warfare
Discover how to abide in the presence of God and break free from the grip of enchantments and spiritual oppression. In this powerful sermon drawn from Psalm 91, we explore what it truly means to dwell under the shadow of the Almighty. Learn practical, biblical steps to presence of God walk in authority, build spiritual altars, and sustain a lifestyle filled with the Word, prayer, and power. This message offers deep insight into overcoming divination and enchantment—not by fear, but by the presence of God and the power of His Word.
Topics covered:
• Combating enchantments through the Word
• Living as a carrier of God’s presence
• The power of spiritual altars and confession
• How to build hunger for the Word
• Walking in purity, boldness, and spiritual dominion
Perfect for believers seeking to grow in spiritual maturity and authority.
