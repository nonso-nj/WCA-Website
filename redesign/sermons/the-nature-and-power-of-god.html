Sermon

The Nature and Power of God

April 20, 2025Bro Tope Williams

Download sermon notes (PDF)

Hebrews 1:1-4 KJVS
God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, [2] Hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son, whom he hath appointed heir of all things, by whom also he made the worlds; [3] Who being the brightness of his glory, and the express image of his person, and upholding all things by the word of his power, when he had by himself purged our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high; [4] Being made so much better than the angels, as he hath by inheritance obtained a more excellent name than they.

"You are not ordinary—because the Spirit of God lives in you. His power is in you. His holiness is in you. And in these last days, God is raising a generation that walks in purity and power, light and authority. But here's the key: you cannot walk in divine power without embracing divine nature. Holiness isn’t optional—it’s the gateway to intimacy with God and effectiveness on earth. Be holy, for He is holy."
In this powerful message, we dive deep into the Nature and Power of God, discovering what it means to live as partakers of the divine nature in a dark and perverse world. Learn how holiness unlocks spiritual power, why staying connected to the source—Jesus—is critical, and how to overcome the enemy’s tactics of corruption and distraction.
Featuring scriptures from Hebrews 1, 1 Peter 1, 2 Peter 1, Ephesians 1 & 6, and more, this message will equip you to live boldly, purely, and powerfully in Christ.
