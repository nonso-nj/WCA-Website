Sermon

Exhortations from Various ministers.

June 19, 2022Bro Achu Bisong, Bro Emeka Anosike, Bro Femi Bankole, Sis Udeme Anosike

Listen

How is it then, brethren? when ye come together, every one of you hath a psalm, hath a doctrine, hath a tongue, hath a revelation, hath an interpretation. Let all things be done unto edifying.

1 Corinthians 14:26 KJV

2 Timothy 2:19 KJV Nevertheless the foundation of God standeth sure, having this seal, The Lord knoweth them that are his. And, Let every one that nameth the name of Christ depart from iniquity. The Lord knows in the heart those who are following Him and He also has a reward for those who play games. Regardless of peoples belief, He prepares a recompense for those who are on the earth. The Journey with the Lord is a process. You do not jump ship. Studying daily and frequently is important for a student in school. The one who waits till the last minute has his reward after examination, when his/ her grades come out. Matthew 25:1-10 speaks of virgins (which speaks of virgin hearted believers). Some where wise and others were foolish. God does not speak in scriptures as an abuse, but on a descriptive level. These foolish did not take time to gather what was needed to walk with the Lord in the long run. A person who decide not to follow through with God's warning for character transformation, may at some point in life have people reach out for a fruit, but find none. God always tests our commitments to Him. Matthew 25:6. Stay focused! There is a reward for following God.
