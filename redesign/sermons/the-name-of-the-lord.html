The Name of the Lord

March 28, 2019Sis Beulah Bisong

Listen

Audio coming soon

Download sermon notes (PDF)

  • The name of the Lord as Authority.

The word name in the Hebrew scriptures is ‘shem’, which stands for authority. The name of the Lord is the authority of the Lord. As believers when you have been translated from the kingdom of darkness into the kingdom of God, Colossians 1:13, you have certain rights and privileges. However, it is knowledge and faith that gives us access to it. Psalm 49:20, Galatians 4:1. We have to know that the name is powerful and understand what Jesus has done and why it is powerful to utilize effectively. The name has been freely given to the church as long as you are a believer, it requires that we grasp an understanding, to know this absolutely to use it.

A case study to understanding this authority will be from the book of Esther 3. 7- 15. “ And the king took his ring from his hand, and gave it unto Haman the son of Hammedatha the Agagite, the Jews’ enemy … in the name of King Ahasuerus was it written, and sealed with the king’s ring”. We could see from the story above the power of the kings’ ring. The entire nation knew the power of the kings’ signet ring, symbolizing his authority. Haman could write anything and seal it with the kings’ signet ring and it was taken as the word of the King since it was sealed in his name and with his signet ring. The king did not have to know about Haman’s’ decree for it to be law. It just has to be sealed in his name. In a similar way, we have the name of the Lord, which everything in heaven and on the earth and beneath the earth recognize and submit to. Matthew 28:18, Philippians 2:9-11. Anything you say in prayer in Jesus’ name is done. We do not need to go back to God to get approval and authority over prayers when He has given us this authority already.

In Esther 8, we can see Esther’s response to the king. She sought to reverse that which Haman had decreed, but that could not be done. The king gave a response “Write ye also for the Jews, as it liketh you, in the king’s name, and seal it with the king’s ring: for the writing which is written in the king’s name, and sealed with the king’s ring, may no man reverse. Esther 8:8. In a similar way, we should have confidence, seeing the name of Jesus as His signet ring, when we pray in the name of Jesus.

Jesus said, And in that day ye shall ask me nothing. Verily, verily, I say unto you, Whatsoever ye shall ask the Father in my name, he will give it to you. John 16:23. We do not need to meet Him for another authority when we ask in His name. We see that only the Lion of the tribe of Jesus can lose that which has been sealed by God, Revelation 5, and if He says He will do what we ask, then we should have the confidence to use His name. No other power can cancel that which is sealed in the name of Jesus. Jesus is described as the Amen, Revelations 3:14. Everything in His name is verily and done. God knew we would need as a powerful tool, so He highly exalted the name Jesus above every name named even in the ages to come. Ephesians 1:21

  • Having the name as an identity/ a seal upon our forehead.

Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, have we not prophesied in thy name? and in thy name have cast out devils? and in thy name done many wonderful works? And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity. Matthew 7:22-23.  A question that could pop in our minds would be, how can people prophesy in God’s name and do miracles and Jesus says, they work iniquity (lawlessness) and He never knew them? There is a distinction between using the name of the Lord and having the name of the Lord written in your forehead, as an identity. Revelations 22:4. Having His name as a seal on your forehead means, you are God’s own. We are to desire this as believers. In the kingdom of God, there are distinctions. Looking at Ezekiel 9:4-6, we see a sealing of some, out of God’s people.  And the LORD said unto him, Go through the midst of the city, through the midst of Jerusalem, and set a mark upon the foreheads of the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations that be done in the midst thereof. Ezekiel 9:4. This sealing was done out of Jerusalem, a called people, starting from His sanctuary, meaning not all in Jerusalem were sealed. In Revelations 7:1-8, we see a similar situation, a sealing of some out of the tribes of Israel. God doesn’t seal you when you’re perfect, but He looks at your heart and if He sees that your heart is crying out to be righteous, to hate every form of wickedness, He places a mark of ownership upon you. 2 Timothy 2:19 says, Nevertheless the foundation of God standeth sure, having this seal, The Lord knoweth them that are his. And, Let every one that nameth the name of Christ depart from iniquity. Those that have the seal of the Lord are those that are His, those who depart from iniquity.

  • The name of the Lord as a defence

The name of the Lord is a strong tower; The righteous runs to it and is safe and set on high [far above evil]. Proverbs 18:10 (AMP). The word ‘tower’ here signifies a high place, an elevated surface. Once we call upon the name of the Lord it is like He has carried us on eagles’ wings far above principalities and powers, to heavenly heights. In Ezekiel 9 and Revelations 7, there was a judgement to be executed, but it did not begin until the seal had been placed on God’s own. The name places us in a place of safety far from calamities and evil. It keeps us in the heavens constantly. We run into the fortress of God when we call upon the name of the Lord. It is our hiding place. Romans 10:13.

  1. Satan trembles at the mention of the name.

It is easy for us to get familiar with the name of Jesus because we use it frequently, but the devils are subject to and fear the name of Jesus. Luke 10:17. When the apostles began to preach, out of fear, the authorities commanded them not to teach in that name. They feared the name.

Colossians 2:10-15 speaks of the victory of Christ over the enemy.

“When He had disarmed the rulers and authorities [those supernatural forces of evil operating against us], He made a public example of them [exhibiting them as captives in His triumphal procession], having triumphed over them through the cross”.

Colossians 2:15 [AMP]

The Message translation puts it in an interesting way.

“He stripped all the spiritual tyrants in the universe of their sham authority at the Cross and marched them naked through the streets”

Colossians 2:15[MSG}.

Satan and the powers of darkness will never forget the day they were put to a public spectacle. He knows the victory has been won and when we say the name of Jesus, we appropriate the victory. Satan remembers that He was destroyed at the cross. Hebrews 2:14-15. He is afraid when we mention the name of God.

  • The name of the Lord allows us to speak as one having authority.

And it came to pass when Jesus had ended these sayings, the people were astonished at his doctrine: For he taught them as one having authority, and not as the scribes.

Matthew 7:28-29.

Having the name of the Lord helps us not only to pray as one having authority but to teach the word as one having authority. Using the word of God and sealing it with the name of the Lord is powerful. It is a guaranteed prayer already, Isaiah 55:11. In the example of Moses and the red sea in Exodus 14:14-16, God told him to stop crying and use the rod (the word also means sceptre, symbolizing power and authority). He had already been given the authority. We are to just speak the word and use the name, Jesus. We are to speak life into situations and seal it with the name of Jesus.

Knowing this, we should not pray and be agitated like the heathens do, but pray knowing we have the backing and authority of the almighty God. Matthew 6:7-8. His name is I AM that I AM, this is a blank cheque. Exodus 3:14, God is our ever-present help in time of need, Psalm 46:1 and is to us what we need: healer, teacher, Father, Friend, Lover, Strength, Salvation, Righteousness, Sanctifier. You name it, He is!

Therefore, my people shall know my name: therefore, they shall know in that day that I am he that doth speak: behold, it is I. Isaiah 52:6

The Grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your Spirit!
