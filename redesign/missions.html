To the nations

Sister ministries

We stand with ministries that carry the gospel of the Kingdom across Canada and around the world.

“His dominion shall be from sea to sea.” Psalm 72:8

Canada Interior Mission

Proclaims the gospel of the Kingdom of Jesus Christ across Canada and the nations, that He alone may be Head and centre of His church. Mission journeys have taken the team across Canada, Europe and the Mediterranean, strengthening churches and planting new fellowships.

Upcoming journeys Visit the website

The gospel of the Kingdom, one city at a time.

Rekindle Hub Global

Exists to awaken, disciple and equip a generation to take their place in the purposes of God. Through Basileia Power Invasion outreaches in cities across Africa and North America, and the Basileia Invasion Community that meets online every Saturday, Rekindle calls believers back to hunger for Jesus, honour for the Word and faith in the power of God.
