The Power of Bearing Fruit for God

June 23, 2025Winnipeg Christian Assembly1 min read

“By this My Father is glorified, that you bear much fruit; so you will be My disciples.”
— John 15:8 (NKJV)

Have you ever wondered how we, as followers of Christ, can truly glorify God? Jesus gives us the answer right here: it’s by bearing much fruit.

But what does it mean to bear fruit? It means to live a productive, Spirit-led life. It’s allowing the Holy Spirit to work in and through us until His character and nature are expressed in ways that others can see.

The world may never see God Himself, but it can certainly see him revealed in the results of His work within us. These results (i.e, “fruits”) aren’t born out of striving or human effort. They’re called the fruit of the Spirit because they’re the evidence of yielding to Him. Our role is not to strain harder, but to submit deeper to His leading.

Jesus Himself set the example. Everywhere He went, the fruit of obedience and intimacy with the Father was evident in how He loved, served, and brought glory to God, and He invites us to walk in the same way.

Often, when we think of bearing fruit, we imagine only the “big” moments like preaching the Gospel, winning souls, and performing miracles. Those things are beautiful, but God also delights in the small moments like extending kindness to a neighbor, choosing integrity in a challenging situation, and forgiving when it’s hard. These are the daily ways we bear fruit and bring glory to Him.

Reflection:

What fruit is God calling you to bear in this season?

Are you submitting to the Holy Spirit in the big and small moments of your day?

Will you make it your priority to glorify the Father by allowing His character to be revealed in your life?

Prayer:
Lord, thank You for choosing me to bear fruit for Your glory. Help me to remain connected to You — the True Vine — so that my life can reflect Your heart. Teach me to surrender every area of my life to the Holy Spirit, allowing Him to produce fruit that shines for You. In Jesus’ Name, Amen.
