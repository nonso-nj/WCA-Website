A Glorious War: Fight like God II

August 7, 2021Winnipeg Christian Assembly4 min readNo. 67

Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might. [11] Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil. [12] For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places. [13] Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand.

Ephesians 6:10-13 KJV

Every King who has sons makes it a duty to train them to be in the army. From a young age, he begins to train his princes because he knows the Kingdom will be committed into their hands. Man was made as a son of God. The bible says the heavens of the heavens is the Lord, but He gave the earth to the sons of Men, Psalm 115:16. It is for this reason that our Father brings us through various battles that we may learn to fight and master the use of each weapon He has provided for us.

Yesterday, we began studying the weapon of our warfare and concluded with the breastplate of our salvation. We discussed that our enemy has been stripped of power, however, he has wiles and schemes. Our weapons are therefore effective against these wiles that he has successfully used to destroy many. These weapons receive their power and might from God Himself, hence taking up the armor of God is taking up the strength of God to war. The enemy dwells in darkness and thrives where there is ignorance. Therefore to save man, God had to introduce Light into the world. This light is the gospel, light unto salvation, 2 Corinthians 4:4. Though foolish to the natural man, it is the power of God that can set free a man who is depraved and bound for destruction. It is the only thing that can convert a serial killer to a saint, completely.

Gospel of Peace – In the armour of God, the gospel of peace is given for shoes. The shoes used in the Roman Armoury were called the Caligae. It was a shoe with hobnails, having nails for its sole. It gave stability anywhere and allowed the soldiers to cut through steep slopes. The gospel of peace is the gospel of Jesus Christ, the good news that we are in union with God. Jesus said, His peace has the ability to keep a heart from being troubled in a troubled world, John 14:27. Isaiah 52:7 says that the feet of the people who bring good news are beautiful because they show the way of peace to all men. This Gospel in itself is the power of God unto salvation. In Luke 10:1-21, Jesus called the disciples and told them to go and preach the gospel. He told them to cast out demons and heal the sick and set the captives free by the gospel. They returned, happy that the demons were subject to them. Jesus however, told them to rejoice since their name was written in heaven. In 2 corinthians 4:3-12, Paul said he was troubled on every side, cast down yet not destroyed. Why? Because he had a treasure, the power of God in his vessel. In releasing this gospel, the life of God was made manifest in his body. The testimony within him preserved him, saved him from storms and shipwrecks till it was complete. We see a similar case in the book of revelation 11:6-7, while the two witnesses had their testimony, they could not be harmed until their witness was complete. The same happened for our Lord Jesus, John 7:30. Preaching the gospel not only saves the one who hears but even the one who preaches is preserved and blessed.

Shield of Faith – The shield on the Roman soldier was used to cover their whole body. They used it to defend themselves against the arrows and attacks of the enemy. In our case, these fiery darts are words as Psalm 64:3 says. They are words aimed to kill and destroy. Recall that this armor is the armor of God, this means the faith here is the faith of the Son of God and not yours. It is more than the Hebrews 11 faith, it is the Hebrews 12 faith. The faith that looks unto Jesus and away from that which easily besets us.  This faith is the appropriation of Christ as your everything, life, faith, protection, hope. It is learning to colonize the earth by the realities of your union with Christ. It understands that as He is in the heavens, so are you on earth. Because this faith is so fixated on Jesus, everything else is a lie. To live by the faith of the son of God is to appropriate the relationship God has with Jesus as yours, this is why you are the righteousness of God in Christ Jesus. “Everything I see in Jesus is me, nothing more, nothing less”. All of the devil’s weapons can be destroyed by this one posture.

It is not enough to know these truths, we need to employ these weapons in our daily lives. A classic example would be a case of an accusation from the enemy over a shortcoming. Our faith in the finished of Christ should be quickly raised up to block this fiery dart. If faith is lacking, however, Preaching the gospel to ourselves and rehearsing the truth that we are at peace with God with our mouths till we believe it, is warfare. The idea of learning to take on and use this armory now is such that when the evil day comes (or on the day of adversity), when many fall, you will stand.

Prayer for Today: Lord Jesus thank you for your death on the cross that gave me access to all that you are including your faith that makes me stand. As I go through life today, interacting with friends and coworkers, help me to live out this faith in your finished work and share this good news of peace to them that are troubled. I receive the grace and ability to be a light in the darkness today, living today as You would!
