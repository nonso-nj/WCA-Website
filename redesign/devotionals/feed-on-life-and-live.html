Devotional

Feed on LIFE and live!

April 21, 2025Winnipeg Christian Assembly2 min read

For the life of the flesh is in the blood: and I have given it to you upon the altar to make an atonement for your souls: for it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul. … For it is the life of all flesh; the blood of it is for the life thereof: therefore I said unto the children of Israel, Ye shall eat the blood of no manner of flesh: for the life of all flesh is the blood thereof: whosoever eateth it shall be cut off.»
Leviticus‬ ‭17‬:‭11‬, ‭14‬ ‭KJV‬

In the Old Testament, God told His people not to eat the flesh or drink the blood of animals. Why? Because the life of the animal is in the blood. But when it comes to Jesus, we are invited to eat His flesh and drink His blood, so we can receive His life.

John 6:53-56 KJV
Then Jesus said unto them, Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except ye eat the flesh of the Son of man, and drink his blood, ye have no life in you.
[54] Whoso eateth my flesh, and drinketh my blood, hath eternal life; and I will raise him up at the last day.
[55] For my flesh is meat indeed, and my blood is drink indeed.
[56] He that eateth my flesh, and drinketh my blood, dwelleth in me, and I in him.

What was Jesus referring to when He said we should eat His flesh and drink His blood? He didn’t mean it in a literal or physical way. It means to feed on His Word (read and think deeply about it), to drink from His life-giving Spirit, and to spend time with Him through prayer, worship, and fellowship.

We’re no longer under the old covenant where people had to follow physical laws like not eating blood—but still, the question remains: What are you feeding on?

When we spend time with Jesus—reading the Bible, thinking about His sacrifice, spending time in His presence—we receive His life. But if we’re constantly taking in things that aren’t from Him , things that come from the world or our sinful nature (our “flesh”), which we can call four footed beasts in this context , we are also feeding on that.

What do we spend our time watching, reading, or listening to? What kind of things fill our hearts and minds?

If the things we’re getting life and enjoyment from aren’t connected to Jesus, if they’re not born of the same spirit as Him, we’re feeding our soul with something else. It’s like eating the “flesh and blood” of something that’s not of God. And over time, that can pull us away from Him.

Leviticus warns us not to eat the blood of any flesh. Today, we can see “flesh” as the sinful, selfish part of us that doesn’t want God. If the things we feed on (mentally, emotionally, spiritually) are born from that kind of flesh, then they will only grow our sinful nature.

But Jesus gives us an invitation, He says that if we drink His blood and eat His flesh, we shall have His life. King James uses ‘eateth’ and ‘drinketh’, which is the old English style of using the present continuous, it’s something we do all the time. As we continually drink His blood and feed on His flesh by engaging His Spirit, engaging His word, meditating on the power of His blood and His salvation, He feeds us with more of His life!

Reflection:
– What do I spend the most of my time on? Meditating on the Word of God? Fear? Envy? Malice?
– How can I better engage my mind to meditate on the true life and live?

Jesus, thank You for giving Your life for me. Help me to feed on Your Word and stay close to You.
Fill me with Your Spirit and keep me from the things that pull me away.I want Your life in me, more and more each day. Amen.
