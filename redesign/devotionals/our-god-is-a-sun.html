Our God is a Sun

September 8, 2025Winnipeg Christian Assembly2 min read

Have you ever looked up at the starry sky and wondered: why doesn’t everything just fall down? Physicists tell us that the planets aren’t static — they orbit the Sun. The Sun contains about 99.8% of the total mass of our solar system and sustains all life on Earth. Because of its immense gravity, everything is constantly being pulled toward it. The only reason Earth hasn’t fallen into the sun is because of its sideways orbital speed that keeps it in moving around the sun. Amazing, isn’t it?

Scientists rightly say life depends on the Sun. Without it, Earth would be a frozen rock drifting in darkness. But we know it’s not really the Sun that sustains us — it’s the God who made it. Scripture says:

“He upholds the universe by the word of His power.” (Hebrews 1:3, ESV)
“The Lord God is a sun and shield.” (Psalm 84:11, ESV)
“The Sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its wings.” (Malachi 4:2, ESV)

Like the Sun pulls the planets in, our God pulls us towards Himself and His righteousness. He is constant, faithful, and the source of all life. Have you ever battled sin or addiction and thought, this pull is too strong, I can’t resist it? Romans 5:26 tells us that the pull of God’s grace is infinitely stronger than the pull of sin. Paul reminds us:

“He will sustain you to the end, guiltless in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ. God is faithful…” (1 Corinthians 1:8–9, ESV)

So why do we still struggle if His pull is so strong? Think of Earth orbiting the Sun: it is always being pulled inward, but its sideways motion keeps it circling instead of falling in. In the same way, like Adam hid after he sinned, sin and shame make us “turn aside” from God, running in circles instead of drawing near. Some of us “orbit faster” than others — running harder after our own ways — but God’s pull never stops. We can choose to keep resisting, or we can turn and face Him in full surrender. The moment we turn toward the Son, His pull becomes irresistible.

God is patient. Through Jeremiah He said:

“I have sent to you all my servants the prophets… saying, ‘Turn now every one of you from his evil way.’” (Jeremiah 35:15, ESV)

Imagine His heart — looking at us day after day, saying, maybe today my son will turn, maybe today my daughter will yield.

But make no mistake: a day is coming when every knee will bow and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord (Philippians 2:10–11). In that day, Isaiah 30 says the strength of the sun’s light will be seven times stronger! Still the greatest treasure remains for those who gladly seek Him NOW!

Jesus said:

“I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will not walk in darkness.” (John 8:12, ESV)

Will you keep running in circles, or embrace the Son’s light and be free?

Prayer: Lord, I am tired of running around, I choose to be still and know You as my God! Help me to yield to Your grace and Your power! You said You are able to keep me strong till the end, free from all blame until Christ returns and that You are faithful to do this because You always keep Your Word! Firm and constant as the Sun! Be it unto me according to Your Word! I ask in Jesus name, Amen.
