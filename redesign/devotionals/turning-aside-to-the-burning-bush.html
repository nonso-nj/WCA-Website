Turning Aside to the Burning Bush

June 4, 2025Winnipeg Christian Assembly3 min read

“And Moses said, I will now turn aside, and see this great sight, why the bush is not burnt.…’”
Exodus 3:3 (KJV)

We often feel stuck—prayers unanswered, seasons of waiting, or routines that leave us restless. Yet God’s mercy can break into the most ordinary moment and redirect us into His purpose. Moses, once a prince turned fugitive, was tending sheep when he saw a bush aflame yet unharmed. In that instant, his wilderness became a sacred threshold.

Reflection

1. Encountering the Unseen Flame
A bush burning without being consumed shows that God’s refining fire preserves rather than destroys. When trials surround you—financial strain, broken relationships, unanswered prayers, remember God is at work beneath the surface. Your “flames” are not meant to devour but to make you aware of His presence.

2. The Courage to Turn Aside
“Moses, Moses!” God’s voice called softly. To “turn aside” is more than a brief pause; it’s a faith-filled choice to hush our agendas and listen. If a verse suddenly stirs your heart or a burden nudges your spirit, stop. Whisper, “Here am I,” and create space for God’s strategy to become clear.

3. Removing What Separates Us
God told Moses to remove his sandals. Beyond cultural etiquette, those sandals represent anything that separates us from intimacy with God. This can be pride, distractions, unconfessed sin, or unresolved pain. Each morning, we put on mental “sandals” (to-do lists, self-reliance). Laying them down means humbling ourselves so any place—our kitchen, office, or car, becomes holy ground.

4. Hearing the Name “I AM”
God revealed His name, “I AM THAT I AM” (Exodus 3:14), assuring Moses of His eternal, unchanging nature. Moses’s doubt, “Who am I?”, met God’s promise: “I AM with you.” In your doubts—“I’m not capable,” “I lack resources”, remember you operate in the power of the One who exists beyond time. His “I AM” is your guarantee that He will equip you.

5. From Exile to Appointment
Moses’s detour was not wasted; it was preparation. His calling, to deliver a nation, emerged only after he stood before the burning bush. Likewise, your seasons of waiting or wandering are God’s workshop, shaping you for greater things. As Romans 8:28 reminds us, “All things work together for good to those who love God.”

Reflection Questions

  • What “flame” is God using to get your attention right now?
  • Which “sandals” separate you from intimacy with Him? Pride, distraction, or unconfessed sin?
  • How can you “turn aside” today and say, “Here am I”?

Prayer
Lord Jesus, I confess that I often rush past Your gentle call. Like Moses, I carry “sandals” that keep me from drawing near, whether it is pride, distraction, and unforgiveness. Today, I choose to lay them down. I pause, turn aside, and say, “Here am I.” Reveal Your “I AM” over my doubts. Equip me for Your purpose and transform my detours into divine appointments. In Jesus's mighty Name, Amen.

Action Steps

  1. Identify Your “Sandals”: Today, ask the Holy Spirit to reveal anything that separates you from intimacy with God: habits, attitudes, or unconfessed sin. Write them down, pray for grace to surrender them, and commit to remove them as soon as you wake.
  2. Create a Daily “Holy Ground” Moment: Each morning, set aside ten minutes without phone or distractions. Remove anything hindering your connection. This could be mental to-do lists, worry, or shame. Quietly ask the Lord, “What do You want me to hear today?” Record any impressions.

When you remove what separates you from God and turn aside to listen, any ordinary moment can become holy ground. May you answer, “Here am I,” as He transforms your detour into destiny. Amen.
