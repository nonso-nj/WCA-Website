A Glorious War – Know thine enemy

August 5, 2021Winnipeg Christian Assembly4 min readNo. 65

Part 1 of 3 in A Glorious War

Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might. [11] Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil. [12] For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.

Ephesians 6:10-12 KJV

The command of God to man in Genesis 1:26 to subdue the earth and have dominion was an indicator that there were things that were in opposition to man. It was no wonder that little after Adam appeared on the scene, the serpent showed up as an enemy. This serpent has been and still is the enemy of every man that has lived and is alive. All men are born into a battlefield, whether or not they are aware of it. The man in Christ has been delivered from slavery to the enemy and has been translated into the kingdom of God. However, because man has not yet dominated the earth and subdued it, the battle remains.

When people speak of spiritual warfare these days, especially in countries with a lot of history in dark witchcraft, there is often a false magnification of the enemy which produces fear and decapacitates the fighter. This fear often creates fear-filled and paganistic prayers. It is important to note that man, no matter how wicked they may seem is not the enemy, the devil is the one we war against. Jesus was made manifest to destroy all the works of the devil, 1 John 3:8. Satan has been defeated quite alright, but he is one that thrives in darkness or ignorance. Hence much of our spiritual warfare is a battle for true knowledge. Ephesians 6:10-12 speaks of the wiles of the devil. His powers are just that, wiles and schemes. It is therefore important to know some of these schemes and how to overcome them.

Before that, it is important that we clear some misconceptions about the enemy. Outside the fact that his powers have been destroyed, Satan is neither omnipresent nor omniscient. He did not know when Mary was pregnant with Jesus or who the baby was, else he would have moved Herod to specifically target Mary and kill Jesus, Matthew 2. As seen in Job 2:2, he needs to go to and fro the earth. He can also be resisted by a man and when he is resisted, he runs away James 4:7, 1 Peter 5:8-9. He has been stripped of all power, Colossians 2:15, and is a destroyed entity, Mark 16:17-18, 1 John 3:18. He is judged and cast out, John 10:31. You actually have authority over all his powers and there is nothing he can do about it, Luke 10:19. He has a kingdom, but you are not in it. Lastly, he actually needs an opportunity to act in your life. These opportunities can come in various ways like what we watch, listen to, and expose ourselves to, the words of our confession, doubt, etc.

While the devil is not wise, because wisdom belongs to God, he is smart and has over 6000 years of experience deceiving man. If he is able to create a mold, based on culture, an ideology, or something contrary to truth and set a man on that cause to destruction, it frees him to go after others. This is how he has successfully deceived and caused the destruction of many. This is why Paul in 2 corinthians 10:4 says the weapons of our warfare are mighty through God to pull down strongholds and imaginations that exalt themselves above the knowledge of God. Strongholds are ideologies that we may have held on to for a long time that almost become a part of who we are. The enemy is also known as an accuser, Revelation 12:9. This is perhaps the sum total of his ministry and his most potent seat. His ability to accuse is largely due to man’s ignorance or unbelief in the finished work of Christ.

This knowledge of satan’s tricks will help us better appreciate the armor of God in Ephesians 6:10-18, and its ability to keep us standing even with the onslaught of the wiles of the enemy. Again, you are fighting against a defeated foe. However, a lot of the time, Christians are like soldiers with a riffle who leave their guns and a pack of celestial soldiers with them and flee from an unarmed enemy with a very loud mouth. We need to understand that they that are for us are more than they that are against us, Romans 8:31, Psalm 34:7. In the end of all things, when the light of God’s word is fully turned on for all to see, as Isaiah 14:16 says, men will narrowly consider the devil and wonder saying “is this he who troubled the nations”. In the meantime, arm yourself with truth, the Word of God!

Prayer for today: Lord Jesus, thank you for the light of your Gospel that set me free from sin and ignorance. Thank you for sending your light to me every day to enlighten my darkness. Lord please reveal to me any lie that I have believed about myself, You or the situations around me, that is leaving an open door for the enemy. Shine your light and reveal truth in this area to me. Amen.
