Devotional

Prove Me now

June 12, 2021Winnipeg Christian Assembly4 min readNo. 12

Malachi 3:10-12 KJV

“[10] Bring ye all the tithes into the storehouse, that there may be meat in Mine house, and prove Me now herewith, saith the Lord of hosts, if I will not open you the windows of heaven, and pour you out a blessing, that there shall not be room enough to receive it. [11] And I will rebuke the devourer for your sakes, and he shall not destroy the fruits of your ground; neither shall your vine cast her fruit before the time in the field, saith the Lord of hosts. [12] And all nations shall call you blessed: for ye shall be a delightsome land, saith the Lord of hosts.”

The recent flood and snow blizzard occurrence in Texas sheds some light on what God is saying in verse 10 — “… that there may be meat in Mine house …”. It was a scenario that had not happened in Texas for about 100 years. No one was prepared for it. And when it happened, many people lost everything: their houses, cars, furniture, possessions, etc including jobs, as many office buildings were also affected. Now imagine someone in that situation — they’ve lost everything. What do they do?

In the world, there is probably no hope. But God did not want it so for anyone who is part of the body of Christ (Church). Because, biologically speaking, if any part of the body has an issue, all parts of the body get involved in one way or the other to channel resources to that part in need, till it is fully healed and recovered. Likewise in the church. It should never be that a child of God is stranded, because by God’s design there should be meat (provision) in His house. That is, God intends that His church would always have the necessary resources to meet the need of anyone in the Church, so that no one is stranded.

For example, in the case of the Texas disaster, if a person is affected, the church he is part of should be able to meet his need so that he is not stranded. Or if the entire church is affected, then churches in other states or locations should be able to send resources to assist the brethren in the affected church to recover, so that the people of God are not stranded. This is the same thing we see Paul advocating for in Romans 15:25-26; collecting from the Churches in Macedonia and Achaia to supply to the persecuted Church in Jerusalem.

But how is this possible?

By doing what God said: “Bring ye all the tithes into the storehouse …”. This is how resources are to be available in the church to cater to people’s needs so that no one in the church is stranded. In the days of this passage (Malachi 3:10) people were mainly farmers, and as such the earnings were the harvest. Meaning, their tithe (their ten percent) were food crops that were part of their harvest. That is why God asked them to bring them to the storehouse. So that if any Israelite, or even stranger, ran to the temple of God for help, there would never be a case where the person does not get the assistance they need. But in our current days, our earnings are in money, e.g. salary, contract payment, etc. Our tithe is thus ten-percent of every money we earn.

Although, it should be noted that the fuller dimension of the tithe principle is that we (God’s people now) would be a tithe unto God out of all humanity — those whom God would inherit as His eternal possession to make up His house (1 Peter 2:5, Revelation 21:2-3) with which He will express Himself visibly to all creation.

Notwithstanding, the word tithe is a from a Hebrew word that actually means ten percent. This is how God designed that resources should be available in His house (His church). Not so that anyone man can own it or squander it. But so that no one in the church is ever in a stranded or financially embarrassing situation. Meaning, by faithfully giving your tithe of every money you earn (which is the minimum giving you can do), and choosing rather to only live on at most ninety percent of your earnings, you are making it possible for God’s house to function properly as a healthy body, and to maintain the testimony of being a house of refuge.

Because of how important this is to God, He made a bold statement. He said in verse 10: “… prove Me now …”. That’s a big deal. The God who created heaven and earth is saying to you prove Me. Or as some may say: Try Me. And what are we to prove? He is telling us that if we faithfully give a minimum of our tithe (so that there are resources for the needy in His house) then He will unfailingly open the windows of heaven and pour out a blessing unto us, so much that we won’t have enough room to receive it. “Opening the windows of heaven” simply means that He will give us heavenly spiritual assistance to obtain blessings that are beyond our own efforts; blessings that are by the mighty favour of God working in the hearts of people for our advantage. Part of this heavenly spiritual assistance includes that He will stop unfortunate (resource-draining) situations that lead to loss from happening to us. And He even said because of this His blessing, all nations will call us blessed.

This is what He is asking us to prove, and it shows how important it is to Him that there are resources (funds) in His church to cater for the needy. Let us therefore prove Him to see that He will do as He said He will. And beyond seeking to receive (though He will never fail in His promise) let us also have a heart like His heart to cater for the needy, so that no one in God’s house (and no one who runs to God’s house) would be or remain stranded.

Prayer for today

O Lord, as you have requested, help me to faithfully and sacrificially give a tithe of all my earnings to your Church, so that resources would always be available to meet different needs in Your house, so that Your house shall indeed always be a house of refuge.
