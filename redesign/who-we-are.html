What we believe

01

The Lord God

We believe in one God, eternal and the creator of all things. By Him and for Him all things were created, and He made the worlds (Colossians 1:16; Hebrews 1:2). He alone has immortality, dwelling in the light which no man can approach (1 Timothy 6:15–16), and He is the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ (2 Corinthians 1:3). He has appointed a day in which He will judge the world in righteousness (Acts 17:31).

02

The Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, the visible expression of the invisible God (Colossians 1:15), who came in the flesh, lived without sin, and was made sin for us that we might be made the righteousness of God in Him (2 Corinthians 5:21). We are saved by grace, and that salvation is in stages: our spirit is saved, our soul is being saved, and at the last our body shall be fashioned like unto His glorious body (1 Thessalonians 5:23; Philippians 3:21). He is the hope of every believer, for when He appears, we shall be like Him, because we shall see Him as He is (1 John 3:2).

03

His church, the growth of every believer

We believe the church is the expression of Christ on earth: His bride and His body (1 Corinthians 12:27; Ephesians 5:22–32), married into His estate of life and glory and destined to mirror the exact measure of His life and power. John saw the Lord standing among seven golden candlesticks, and was told they are seven churches (Revelation 1:12–20); the lamps of the candlestick are the seven Spirits of God (Revelation 4:5; Isaiah 11:2), so the church as a body is to express the same fullness of the Spirit that Christ did. He gave gifts to men and every man is an heir of God (Ephesians 4:8; Romans 8:17), so we are committed to the growth of every believer through discipleship, teaching and Bible study, that every joint supply, and the body build itself up unto the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ (Ephesians 4:13, 16).

04

The call, missions

We believe in the preaching of the gospel: go ye into all the world, and teach all nations (Mark 16:15; Matthew 28:19). This is our own backyard as much as the nations: street evangelism, prison ministry, the bus, and the sending of workers and planting of churches wherever the Lord leads. We are a mission-centric church, with different arms through which we express the burden the Lord has given us.

How we are led

A plural eldership

No one man has all sufficiency to administer life to the house of God, except the Lord Jesus Christ. So we do not emphasize singularity of leadership, but have a plural eldership as the principal expression of the leadership of God in the assembly.

Eldership is not defined by physical age, but by spiritual maturity: the elder teach and lead the younger.
