Message summary

The Bread of His Presence

 We have been dealing a lot with types and shadows and we know that the tabernacle of Moses was a structure of worship that God instructed Moses to set up for the people of Israel where He can meet with them. This meditation will hinge on one piece of furniture in the tabernacle, the table of show bread. We will look at the bread and the table and hope to consider some truths from this. Scriptures that deal with the making of this structure, Exodus 25:23-30. The bread of the presence was placed before the Lord always, hence it was also called the bread of the presence.  Also, Leviticus 24:5-9 speaks of the making of the bread. The Lord gave specific instructions on how to make these and we know that there is nothing that the Lord does that is meaningless; they all have applications that are relevant in this time. We will consider the significance of these, in this meditation. 

Jesus called Himself the bread of life, John 6:35. He said if you eat of Me, you will not hunger and if you drink of me, you will never thirst. These things are a shadow of Jesus, pointing to Jesus. If we have accepted Jesus as our personal Lord and Saviour and Jesus lives, then it means that these things have application in our lives today. If the bread speaks about Jesus, then the bread also speaks of the Word, because Jesus is the Word of God, John 6:54-63, John 1:1,11. The primary word of God that we have available to us right now is the logos, the Spirit- inspired scriptures. We also have that which the Lord reveals to us in our spirit by His Spirit dwelling in us. Every word of God is Jesus and as we appropriate these in our lives, we are appropriating the realities of Christ in our lives.  The table of shew bread is always in the presence of God as a witness, Exodus 25:30. There is a power that a thing carries by reason of it being in the presence of God. In Luke 1:19, Gabriel came to meet Zechariah to give him good tidings regarding his son, when Zechariah doubted, Gabriel said he is one that stands in the presence of God. He was coming by the backing of the presence of God with His power. The Word is in the presence of God and comes with the authority and power of His presence. If we put God in remembrance of His word, we obtain justification. Isaiah 43:25-26. God’s word stands as a witness that must always be set before Him, just as the priests always set the bread before the Lord. We can pick any word of scripture and bring it before the Lord as a reminder of His promise. Justification is the next step to glorification. Romans 8:30 says they that He predestined, He called, speaking of another level, then He justified and after justification, the hope is glorification.  Our Lord Jesus has experienced glorification and we will too because we are joint heirs with Christ, Romans 8:17. 

We have established that the bread is the Word of God. The table is the platform in which the Word is served. Psalm 23:5. The Lord is not only making us partakers of the bread but an expression of the bread and the table. When we speak about being able ministers of the word, we are coming into the perfection of the table experience. God has magnified His word above His name, Psalm 138:2, hence we cannot joke with His Word. We must read it, review it, meditate on it, we must rehearse it and it must be our reality everyday. The word of God is our defence, our justification and even our weapon.  Judges 7:13 said that a bread entered the camp and everything scattered, the bread speaking of the word of God. Glorification is our next step, because the work that the Lord is going to do with us in the age to come, He will do with men in a glorified body. There are things that are already glorified, the Father is glorified, Ephesians 1:17, His name is glorified, John 12:28, the son of God is glorified also, Hebrews 1:3, 2:9. However, we are yet to be glorified and the dynamics of our glorification is the glorification of God by us and in us through the set witnesses of the Word and the Spirit. John 13:31-32. How do we glorify the Lord? By an exaltation, a magnification and a lifting up of the name of the Lord in every circumstance. The Spirit and the word are witnesses to us, affirming our sonship in Christ, Romans 8:16. If God is glorified in me, He will also glorify me in Himself. The glorification of God in the lowest state of the dust will lead to Him, drawing you to where He is, a place of glory, John 12:32. Romans 4:18-20 speaks of Abraham who was not weak in faith, but strong in faith giving glory to God. To understand the state at which Abraham believed, assume God says He will give you a good Job in a top engineering firm and you have an engineering degree, there is a basis for hope. However, if He says He will give you a good Job as an engineer in a top firm and you are an illiterate, there is no basis for hope. Abraham was biologically unable to have children and Sarah was biologically unable to carry a child. Yet, He was strong in faith, giving glory to God, knowing who He had believed. This was the secret of Abraham. As we glorify God in ourselves, not by ourselves but through the set witnesses of the Spirit and the Word, He glorifies us in Himself and bestows glory upon us. God has exalted His word and we glorify Him by exalting His promises and His word even amidst other false voices. Psalm 138:2

Considering the table, it was 2 cubits long, one cubit wide and one and a half cubit high. Exodus 25. This was made of two perfect squares speaking of the two set witnesses confirming to us that we are His and He is ours, The Lord dwells in His people by His Spirit and the Word, these witnesses are an authentication of Sonship, Romans 8:16-25. This confidence we have as Sons, helps us to access things pertaining to Sons of God. Acts 15:7-8. The Lord has written His laws in our minds as a witness, Hebrews 10:1-16, however, there is a war that happens in the mind and that is why the conscience can testify against a believer. However, the fact that we know a thing is wrong is a testament to the fact that His laws have been written in our minds, if there is no remorse, that is a sign of a falling away. There is no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus, Romans 8:1. David is an example, committing the act of fornication plus covenant breaking (adultery), lust and murder, yet he said blessed is the man whose iniquity is forgiven, Psalm 32:1-6. He understood that God is his Father, this does not give licence to sin. The heart of a son is remorseful. Psalm 51. However, often time people sorrow and repent from salvation due to guilt, rather than repent to salvation. God first preached salvation to us, by the killing of the lamb in Genesis 3:21 and covering Adam and Eve; showing that the covering for Salvation is of God, Isaiah 30:1, Matthew 24:14. This teaching must have been passed down to Abel by his father, Hebrews 11:4. God witnessed to Him that He was righteous the same way He witnesses to us because of the blood of Jesus that we are righteous. 1 John 5:6-11. As Moses was powerful in administering His work as a servant, even more so, Christ is faithful in administering grace to us, the law was powerful against any transgressor, so also grace is powerful to redeem. However, we must understand our place as a son. Acts 22:15 speaks of people who had been made witnesses. 1 John 1:1-2. By a crystallization of the Spirit of the word in you, you become a witness; it makes you unmovable. Psalm 89:35-37 speaks of Gods promise to Jesus, who is the greater David, and His seed, the church, who has been established as the moon, a faithful witness in heaven. Matthew 5:14-16, Isaiah 60:1-3. Although we are considering the table of shew bread, we must come into the experience of all the furniture’s in the tabernacle, to enter into the fulness of Christ.

In Exodus 25:24, we see that the table is made with wood, but over laid with gold. This speaks of Jesus Christ, humanity overcome with divinity, He is the platform that we must feast from. It is made with crowns round about, representing the kings table. In verse 29, the instruments used to serve, the utensils, are of pure gold. This means that the ministry of the word must be purely from the Spirit, there is no wood, meaning no room for humanity. You cannot teach the people the word of God after receiving inspiration from your carnal sense, that is a ministration of death. We must check our intent and ensure that the inspiration to minister is purity. Frankincense is poured upon the bread, speaking of purity, Leviticus 24:7. It must be from a place of sincerity and truth, not malice and wickedness. 1 Corinthians 5:8. The tables were made with four rings which were used for carrying the table. The two staves speak of the bearers of the word. They are two, representing a company, not one. May the Lord give us grace to be able ministers of the Word. Considering 2 chronicles 35:1-3, Josiah is a type of Christ, who has kept our Passover for us, 1 Corinthians 5:7, and He has set priests to keep the charges and He encourages us in the service of the Lord. The temple of Solomon represents the final resting place of the Lord. You are the temple of God, hence the crystallization of the ark, containing the Spirit and the word of God, would no longer be a burden to you. Josiah was seeking to restore the true temple worship and in 2 chronicles 34:14, Hilkiah the priest found the book of the law, which contained the restoration and establishment plans of the Ark of the covenant, hence opening the dynamics of the final resting place of the Ark of God, the temple of His Body. As a result of this, in Isaiah 22:20-24, his son, Eliakim (meaning God is my portion), was given the government. This is as Jesus Christ, who overcame and was able to open the book that was sealed, Revelations 5:9, hence the ark of God will rest upon His seed, His Church, Revelations 21:3. The Lord has found the book of the Law and has written it in our heart, therefore the realities of Eliakim is our portion. The ark is no longer burdensome to us. The word of God is open to us, we must be a people that always check the books. A time will come when the books (the word of God) becomes so one with you, that it rises up in your spirit. 

In Leviticus 24:7, the bowls and the spoons were filled with frankincense, which was poured on the bread and burnt on the altar, speaking of Jesus living a life of purity; both the purity of the Word and the medium of dispensation. Also, the bread was to be burned with fire, meaning that the word received should be burned (digested) on the altar of prayer. This brings an aroma to the Lord. This is the act of putting the Lord in remembrance, Isaiah 43:26. Before Jesus died, one of the oils that was brought to Him was frankincense, depicting the sweet savour that His death was to bring unto the Lord. We are to be as such before God, 2 Corinthians 2:14-15. If the Word is pure, then the channel also ought to be pure. If this is not the case, we must cry out to God for a cleansing work in us. The table of shewbread contained 12 loaves of bread and all Israel brought it, representing a body ministry; every tribe or joint supplying. 1 Corinthians 10:17. The shewbread typifies the true Bread of Life, John 6:32-35. He was born in a city called Bethlehem, meaning house of bread. The bread was produced with fine flour, wheat, being broken and crushed to the finest form. This means the word broken down in such a way that it can minister to all, even the least. Jesus is the Rose of Sharon, but also the Lily of the Valley, Songs of Solomon 2:1. John 12:24 says except a corn of wheat, which is what the bread is made from, falls to the ground and dies, it abides alone, but if it dies, it brings many fruits. If Jesus has died and resurrected, then this is a law that must be affirmed; our lives must bring forth the fruits in multiple folds. We are the wife of Christ, we must bear fruits in many expressions of the word; in power, character and knowledge. Nahum 2:1 says he that dashes in pieces has come before us, we are to keep the munition (the word of God we have received), watch the way and fortify ourselves mightily (by digging into the word daily). As we eat of the Bread from the Golden table through the Spirit of God, the Life and expression of God becomes a reality. The dynamics of the feasting of the Bread from the table is the key to the Glorification of the saints of God in this time. We must eat from His word and burn (digest) it in the place of prayer until Christ be crystalized or formed in us. 

I am the living bread that came down from heaven. If anyone eats of this bread, he will live forever. And this bread, which I will give for the life of the world, is My flesh.

 John 6:51

May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing!
