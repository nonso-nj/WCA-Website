The I AMs of Jesus

THE FELLOWSHIP IN WINNIPEG- CONFERENCE 2017- BLOW YE THE TRUMPET IN ZION

DAY 3- THE ‘I AM’S’ OF JESUS (MESSAGE SUMMARY)

SPEAKER- SIS EFE OVWAH

Word of prophecy * I hear the desire of your heart longing to be in My presence, but My presence is where you belong. I am constant, I am God almighty. I do what I say I will do. My people, you come and go out of my presence. I am Jehovah Shammah (The Lord that is present). Abide in My presence! In my presence is fullness of Joy and pleasures forever. My presence is the place of Holiness. There is the abomination that makes desolate all around, Stand in the place of Holiness! Come and remain!

Isaiah 55*.

We are in a time and season where the knowledge of Jesus is so low, there is a lack of understanding hence knowledge of truth has been replaced with activities. The Lord Jesus is in the business of revealing Himself to all those who will listen.1 John 5:20. It is necessary that each individual has a revelation of Jesus, that will keep you focused and fruitful. There are many testimonies of who Jesus is from eye witnesses, however, we need to consider most especially, who He said He is. 

Who did Jesus say He is?

  • I am the bread of Life – (John 6:32-35, 48-58)

“And Jesus said unto them, I am the bread of life: he that cometh to me shall never hunger; and he that believeth on me shall never thirst” John 6:35 (KJV)

He said He is the bread, not a bread, of life. As you feed on Him, you receive His life. In explaining this to His disciples, He said the words He speaks are life. John 6:63. We feed on His flesh by ingesting His word. The spirit of the word enters you and enables you to live out His commandments. Read the word, listen to messages, Study His word. What you feed on is what you become!

  • “I am the light of the world” – John 8:12, John 1:4,-5)

“Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life” John 8:12 (KJV)

This current world is in darkness, and can only produce wickedness. Isaiah 60:2. Jesus is the light that enlightens our darkness. John 1:4. He is the light for every and any situation. His light comes with wisdom and understanding. John 18:28. This light grows the more we fellowship with Him. 1 john 1:7.

  • “I am the door” – John 10:1-9

“I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved, and shall go in and out, and find pasture” John 10:9 (KJV)

There are many doors, however, there is only one door that opens to us for our salvation. Acts 4:12. As we enter in, we enter a kingdom of righteousness, peace and Joy in the Holy spirit. Romans 14:17. The Christian experience is one in stages. He opens a door and we find pasture, when we are done in that area, He opens another and keeps opening more till we are brought to perfection. Revelation 3:8,21, Revelation 4:1. 


  • “I am the good shepherd”. – John 10:10-18, Psalm 23

“I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep” John 10:11 (KJV)

The shepherd leads the sheep and is often ahead of the sheep. The sheep follows, not necessarily knowing where, but also no needing to know. He looks at its shepherd. Hebrews 12:2. The shepherd leads with a rod and a staff to both, direct and discipline, to bring to pastures green. Psalm 23:4. In any situation, receive counsel, but let your gaze be on your shepherd. 1 peter 2:25. He knows the way though the wilderness.

  • “I am the resurrection and the Life” – John 11:25-26

“Jesus said unto her, I am the resurrection, and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live: And whosoever liveth and believeth in me shall never die. Believest thou this?” John 11:25-26. 

The Resurrection is a person! And He lives on the inside of you. Romans 8:11. He is eternal life. John 3:6, 1 John 5:20.   Resurrection is a process, as we behold Him in His word and through His Spirit, we are changed to His likeness, 2 Corinthians 3:7-8, and we shall also be changed to be of His very nature. 2 Peter 1:4. Our change from mortality to immortality, corruption to incorruption is not coming from outside, but from within! 1 Corinthians 15:52-54, Philippians 3:20-21. 


  • “I am the way, the truth and the life” – John 14:1-7

“Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me” John 14:6 (KJV). 

This is an emphatic statement that left no room for questioning. The search for truth is a burning question in the minds of every sincere human being, hence the many religions. Truth is a person; hence He is constant. It is knowledge of this truth that will set you free. John 8:32. And if Truth sets free, you are free indeed. John 8:36. Jesus is the only way and in Him is life! 1 John 1:1-2, 1 John 5:20. These are true sayings!

  • “I am the true vine” – John 15:1-10

“I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman” John 15:1 (KJV) . 

Jesus is the true vine and hence, by implication, there is a false vine. If we want to bring the fruits of righteousness, the fruit of the Spirit (Galatians 5:22) , we must remain in Him. John 15:4-5. Fruits of the false vines can be seen in Galatians 5:19-21, heralded by the man of sin, Satan. Its end is death, Romans 6:23, Romans 8:6. Be careful to know where you are receiving your nutrients from. Test and prove all things 1 Thessalonians 5:21. Every truth you hear must glorify Christ and must work in you the fruits of righteousness, else discard it.  

“And we know that the Son of God is come, and hath given us an understanding, that we may know him that is true, and we are in him that is true, even in his Son Jesus Christ. This is the true God, and eternal life” 1 John 5:20 (KJV)

Grace and peace be multiplied to you in the Knowledge of God and Jesus Christ!
