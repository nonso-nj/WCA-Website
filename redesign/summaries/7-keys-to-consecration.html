7 Keys to Consecration

     Consecration is necessary for us to be able to attain to the fulness of life and it is our response to the calling. Titus 2:11-13 tells us that the grace of God brings salvation to us and it also teaches us how to progress in our walk with God towards the fulness of Christ which it will bring us into. This scripture lists how we would go about this progression, speaking of a price we would pay and Jesus Christ in Matthew 16:24-25, also talks about this price as a call to a life of consecration. A life of consecration is required of us as a response if we are to grow into His fulness. How do we practically live a life of consecration to prove we want to go all the way to be like Him?

There are 7 aspects to living a life of consecration;

  • Self denial.
  • Devotions of prayers, praise and worship.
  • Study of God’s word.
  • Practice of God’s word.
  • Practice of self judgement
  • Sharing in the labours of love.
  • Developing a loving disposition in the face of opposition (persecution).

SELF DENIAL

     Matthew 16:24 which we looked at earlier clearly states that we are to deny ourselves if we are to go after Christ. There are 2 aspects to self denial;

  • The first aspect speaks of denial of the world and its lusts. 1John 2:15&16 tells us to draw away from the world and calls us to separate ourselves from anything that will hold us back from moving on in this race. A believer but is still worldly, even though he is saved, hasn’t seen the kingdom. 2Timothy 2:4 says, “No man that warreth entangleth himself with the affairs of this life; that he may please him who hath chosen him to be a soldier”, God has chosen us and called us near to Him, and what He requires of us is to respond to him and do that which will please Him. The things of this world will hold us back and compromise our walk with God, so we should not get ourselves entangled with anything unrighteous and be willing to give up anything the Lord frowns upon.   Galatians 6: 14 and Colossians 3:3 are scriptures that tell us that there should be a clean separation from the world for we are not of this world and God is releasing His help to us, because He is supplying all that we need to get to the Throne so let us receive of Him.
  • The other aspect has to do with certain legitimate things that are not sin in themselves but could still cause stumbling blocks or weights that slow down our pace (1Corinthians 6:12). This aspect isn’t an issue of sin, but because we have seen the glory that is set before us, we don’t want anything to hinder us from going all the way. Practically, we could look at things like what we do with our time, finances and resources, do these things profit and increase us in our walk with God, and contribute to the kingdom? This aspect also involves the choices we make in life, we should depend on the Lord so that we take the right step, especially those of us that are still young, in school, or thinking about marriage. Marriage especially is very important and is not something we just jump into based on looks or even graces, it should be dependent on the Lord because He knows what He wants for us. We must not play with this decision, so we don’t get trapped, because we can change our jobs, careers, where we live or even friends, but not our spouses so we have to get it right. So, we must look at all choices we make, do our jobs take all our time that we don’t have time to serve God adequately? Do we just go into pleasures when God places money in our hands? The kingdom must affect our decisions because God puts us in certain places for the benefit of the kingdom, so we must deny ourselves certain pleasures and desires for the sake of the kingdom.

DEVOTIONS OF PRAYERS, PRAISE & WORSHIP

     Hebrews 4:16 tells us that we are to come boldly, confidently, with assurance to receive at his throne. If we are to become what God wants us to be in Christ Jesus, we must be in communion with Him, therefore, prayers are very important. Ephesians 6:18 and Philippians 1:19 show that it is important that we must give attention to our prayer lives and God will pour upon us, the spirit of supplication, because as God reveals His plans and purposes to us, we are to desire them and reach out to Him in the place of prayer. We must hold onto the Lord for grace to stay in the place of prayer, to pray for ourselves as the Lord opens our understanding more to this vision of the glory. If we do not know how to pray, we will not be able to overcome the present challenges.

     Fasting is an important aspect of prayer and Jesus Christ, speaking in Matthew 6:16, says “Moreover when ye fast” and not if ye fast. When we fast, we are bringing the flesh under and we can wait upon the Lord and concentrate on the burden we are bringing before Him. Therefore, we should include fasting in our regular prayer life, and we shouldn’t wait till when there is a challenge. When we do this, we create an atmosphere, in which the Lord reveals Himself to us. So, if we love the Lord, and are beholding this glory, we should take it upon ourselves to have a time of waiting and seeking God concerning this understanding. It is a lot to take in, and believe we are to be like Christ in this age, but in times of fasting and praying, and staying in the presence of God, He opens much more of that which is ours in this age and in the place of prayers we can hold on to these things in our lives.

STUDY OF GOD’S WORD

     To know God’s will for our lives and the church, we must relate with Him through His Word which is the blueprint. The primary way God speaks to us is through His Word and we must be conversant with the Word of God. All we receive, even of the spirit, must agree with the Word of God, else, it is not of God. So, we must study God’s Word to know His mind and understand the promise that is due to us, else, we don’t know what to pray for. What do we ask for if we don’t know what God is offering us? If we don’t study His Word and understand His instructions and commandments, how do we obey Him? We must study the Word of God in this time because there is so much going on in the world and bringing confusion to those who are not grounded in the Word. So, we must take out time to look again at what comes to us and with a clear understanding of this hope before us, we can pray right and desire it. There is no place for ignorance if we want to come to fulness. The Word of God will guide us, instruct us, and create a hunger in us, so, we should try as much as possible to have a fixed time to study His Word.

PRACTICE OF GOD’S WORD

     We must be obedient to the Word of God and keep His commandment if we say we love Him as John 14:15 tells us. If the Word of the Lord comes to us, and we are obedient to it, the Lord will speak further to us but if we set His Word aside, overtime we will not hear Him because we are doing nothing with the Word that has come to us. Obedience to His Word is a proof that we love Him and that we want to attain to that glory that He has promised us.

PRACTICE OF SELF JUDGEMENT

1Corinthians 13:5.     

     It is good to have self examination of how we are fairing in the light of what we have received, and how we are conducting ourselves. Daily, we should look at how much of our lives we have contributed in bringing glory to God because our lives should bring profit to God, and how we conduct ourselves each time is important for we are living unto God and not ourselves. We should examine ourselves, so that when we find areas of adjustments or shortcomings, we can attend to that area early enough, so we don’t have to restart our journey coming back to the Lord. At the end of every day, we should look over our lives, pray over any shortcomings and receive grace for the next day. We may find out that our week or day has been so busy that we don’t have enough time to go over the scriptures or pray, we should quickly adjust our schedule for the next day or week and not keep pushing it repeatedly. If there is a daily examination, we can call ourselves to order and quickly adjust our program. Matthew 26:41 is a counsel of the Lord to watch and pray, so, let us watch over our lives daily and weekly and we will find that we can keep on the path and not deviate.

SHARING IN THE LABOURS OF LOVE  

     This has to do with our service to God. As said earlier, our lives should bring profit to God, and we are called to be a blessing to the world and the church as the seeds of Abraham (Genesis 26:4). Our appreciation of the love of Jesus Christ to us, is to pledge our lives to His service and as we see in 2Corinthians 5:14&15, if we say we love the Lord, we are called unto a life of service to Him. We could serve Him in the place of praying for the prosperity of the kingdom and it is our responsibility to uphold the work of the kingdom in the lives of men. It is also our responsibility to labour in the place of prayer for our local assemblies where we worship, so that that the Lord will reveal Himself to the assembly. What happens there, is our responsibility and not just that of leader, because we are part of the assembly and it is part of our love, to see the kingdom of God appear, in that assembly. We also have the responsibility, to minister the measure of Christ in us to our local assemblies, to contribute to the growth of Christ in the local assembly. Romans 12:3, tells us that there is a measure of grace that Christ has put in us, that is to contribute to Christ being revealed in our local assemblies, so, we must seek for avenues to express that grace to bring addition to the church. 

     As we faithfully use that gift, it grows into a ministry and eventually brought into the estate of the 5-fold ministry. The 5-fold ministry is a process and as we begin to faithfully flow in the gifts that God gives to us, He will begin to add unto them and there is a growth in us. We shouldn’t go asking God for our ministry, but as we pray and express ourselves in our local assemblies with the gifts He has given us, and identify the challenges in people’s lives, the gifts of the spirit begin to manifest in that line and gradually we build up into a ministry. As much as possible we shouldn’t try to push ourselves into a ministry, but as people identify the gifts in you, they will call for your ministry. We shouldn’t always try to be recognized, because God recognizes what we do and in due time, He will bring us to the limelight. So, we must be humble and patient because the way we operate also affects and hinders us from bringing the best of that grace that is within us, so there must be a submission to the Holy Spirit amid the assembly at any time. Our gift is to bring blessing to the church and to us so if we are using our gifts to create problems, we are not honouring God. Sharing in the labours of love and living our life unto God in His service, is how we pay our debt of love to the Holy Spirit. So, each of us must find our place in our local assembly, to bring addition to the church and glory to God.

DEVELOPING A LOVING DISPOSITION IN THE FACE OF OPPOSITION (PERSECUTION).

     The environment in which we are is hostile to righteousness and there is opposition (Matthew 10:16-18). When we begin to live an uncommon life because of what the Lord is revealing to us, and the desire to be like Him and go all the way, people around us, even some Christians, will begin to wonder what is wrong with us and call us names because they don’t understand the high calling. There must be a loving disposition towards them because they don’t have understanding and they don’t see what we see, therefore, it is important we don’t react negatively. We must receive grace to have a loving disposition because that is the spirit of Christ. God, in 2Timothy 3:12, warns us before hand of this persecution and Romans 8:35 shows us the things we might experience, will these all discourage us from going all the way? Romans 8:18 talks about the glory to be revealed to us being greater than the persecution we will face therefore, we must develop a loving disposition and the Lord will give us grace and help us. 

     There are persecutions that will come when we are walking in the right path for the sake of the Gospel but sometimes there are ways we conduct ourselves that draw unnecessary persecutions. There must be a great dependence on the Holy Spirit for wisdom to help us and as we continue with Him, yielding unto Him and desiring all that He has made available, the Lord will grant us the Spirit of His Son Jesus for us to persevere, go through all temptations and trials, and not compromise.
